Frequently Asked Questions

What types of clients do you work with?

We work with individuals, families, and business owners at various stages of life and wealth accumulation. Whether you are just beginning to build your financial foundation or navigating a complex situation, we have the expertise to help you reach your goals.

How are you compensated?

Sapient Road Wealth Management LLC operates as a fee-based advisor. Our fee structure is transparent and clearly outlined in our client agreement. We believe in aligning our compensation with your success. We do not earn commissions on product sales.

What is the minimum investment required?

We do not impose a rigid minimum investment requirement. Our goal is to serve clients who are committed to working toward their financial future. During our initial consultation we will discuss your situation and determine how we can best serve your needs.

How often will we meet or communicate?

We believe in proactive, ongoing communication. Typically, we meet formally with clients at least twice per year for comprehensive reviews — and we are always available when important life events or market conditions warrant a conversation.

Are you a fiduciary?

Yes. As a registered investment advisor, Sapient Road Wealth Management LLC is held to a fiduciary standard, which means we are legally and ethically obligated to act in your best interest at all times. This is a commitment we take very seriously.

How do I get started?

Getting started is easy. Simply reach out through our contact page to schedule a complimentary initial consultation. There is no obligation — just an honest conversation about your financial future and how we might be able to help.
